# Privacy Policy — Daily Mystery

Daily Mystery stores the following data using Reddit's built-in storage:

- Your Reddit user ID and username, to track scores and show the leaderboard
- Your scores and which cases you have played
- Any mystery cases you create, including their text content

This data is stored on Reddit's platform. It is not shared with, sold to, or
transmitted to any third party. No data is collected outside of Reddit.
